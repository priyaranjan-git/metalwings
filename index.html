Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a trusted leader in indigenous defence and aerospace technologies, strengthening India's security through innovation, engineering excellence, and world-class solutions.

Our Mission

To design, develop, and deliver advanced indigenous defence and aerospace solutions that empower the Armed Forces, support Atmanirbhar Bharat, and build a safer, stronger future for the nation.

WHO WE ARE

Engineering India's strategic edge

Metalwing Defence & Aerospace Pvt. Ltd. is an innovation-driven defence and aerospace company dedicated to advancing India's strategic capabilities through indigenous research, engineering excellence, and next-generation technologies. We design, develop, integrate, and sustain mission-critical defence systems that empower the Armed Forces with reliable, intelligent, and future-ready solutions.

Our expertise spans UAVs, Counter-Drone Systems, Passive RF Detection, Drone Detection Radars, Command & Control Systems, Remote-Controlled Weapon Stations (RCWS), Integrated Perimeter Security Systems, Aviation MRO, Ground Support Equipment, Software Development and Testing, and advanced electrical, electronic, and engineering solutions.

Beyond product development, Metalwing is committed to the indigenisation, modernization, and life-cycle support of defence platforms, reducing dependence on imports while enhancing operational readiness. Every solution we deliver is engineered with precision, built to the highest standards of quality and reliability, and designed to meet the evolving requirements of modern warfare.

With innovation at our core and excellence in every detail, Metalwing Defence & Aerospace Pvt. Ltd. is building the future of defence — strengthening national security through indigenous technology, engineering leadership, and unwavering commitment to those who protect the nation.
